# Refitting the tier-1 passers with inputs the repository already holds (#477, track U of #374)

A scratch measurement under the pressure-day judge as amended by the pull request that closes #407. It writes nothing into
`docs/runs/` and moves no published figure (`docs/decisions/lockbox.md`: scored days 2018-06-29 to 2025-12-31 only; the
confirmation window is not looked at). Declared before any score in `metadata/unused_inputs.json` and, for the judge, in the
fifteen files `metadata/pressure_judge/candidates/<passer>_{settle,dealer,policy}.json`; `scripts/unused_inputs.py` refuses an
uncommitted declaration, and the judge refuses an uncommitted candidate file. Nothing is named for the confirmation look, and every
input stays off in the published declaration.

## What was declared

* **The five passers** are the five tier-1 passers of #428 as `metadata/risk_date_severity.json` declares them: `risk_gbm`,
  `risk_gbm_base`, `risk_logistic`, `risk_logistic_base`, `risk_quantile_skewt_base`. Estimator, risk-date rule, exact-zero forecast on
  other days and inputs are unchanged. Each is re-scored here on the same scratch panel and fold grid, so a candidate's control is its
  own passer and the difference is the group's alone.
* **Three groups**, each added to each passer at every horizon (fifteen candidates):
  * **settle**, settlements-forward: `net_settlement`, `net_settlement_bills`, `net_settlement_due_5d` (#426; public at 12:00 ET on
    the row's own day).
  * **dealer**, dealer and segment: `dealer_treasury_position` (FR 2004, six business days after the as-of date, a column of the
    published panel), and the Fed repo operation fields of #425 (`fed_repo_submitted`, `fed_repo_ops`, `fed_repo_rate_iorb_bps`,
    `fed_repo_log_accepted`, `fed_repo_log_accepted_change`, `fed_repo_days_since_positive`; available 16:00 ET on the next business
    day, the conservative reading).
  * **policy**: the nine `policy_*` columns of #412 (public at 16:00 ET on the row's own date).
* **No new availability guard.** No input is added to the panel, the registries or `contract.py`; every column is read through the
  as-of rule at the lag its own source declares, and the guards behind those reads are already tested. So no new guard and no
  mutation record are owed; `tests/test_unused_inputs.py` pins the declaration (each candidate is its passer plus one declared group,
  every column is collected and off in the published feature map, nothing is named for the confirmation look).

## Inventory (acceptance criterion 1)

`evidence/unused-inputs/inventory.md` is generated by `scripts/unused_inputs.py inventory`: every collected input column, its
source, whether it is in the published feature map, its as-of availability (the registry's release lag) and the declared
candidates that read it today. Not behind a panel column and therefore not a candidate input: the money-fund holdings
(`metadata/sec_nmfp_archives.json`; the extract carries the snapshot's retrieval time, not an as-of date, so there is no as-of read of
it) and the SRF usage snapshots (`srf_inputs`), whose operations enter through `fed_repo_*`. The H.8 snapshot enters through
`bank_total_assets` inside the reserve-scarcity state the passers already use.

(`evidence/unused-inputs/tables.md`, Table 1; `judge.md` is the full report with the regime and pressure-day splits).

**The missed years stay missed.** In 2018 (0 of 5), 2020 (0 of 2) and 2024 (0 of 2) not one of the nineteen models flags an onset at any

intervals of the judge's Table 1 (recall about 0.5 to 0.58 for every row, intervals overlapping).

## What did not run

* `risk_quantile_skewt_base_dealer` could not be fitted at h = 1 and h = 5: the skew-t form's quantile regression is a linear program
  (`sklearn` `QuantileRegressor`, HiGHS) that returns no solution on these unscaled columns (`TypeError: 'NoneType' object is not
  subscriptable`, status 4, numerical difficulties). It is recorded as a failure, dropped at every horizon (the judge needs all five)
  and reported as not scored. Scaling the inputs would change the estimator of the passers and is not done here.
* `ofr_tri_rate` and `ofr_gcf_rate` are left out of the dealer group. They are observed on 1324 and 144 of the 1935 scratch-panel rows
  (none before 2020-09-09 and 2020-10-23: earlier values were filled in after the fact), and the direct design refuses an unobserved
  input; including them would drop every scored day before September 2020, which includes 2018 and 2020, the years under test. A
  missing-value indicator or a zero-fill would be a new design choice. This is flagged for Eleonora below.
