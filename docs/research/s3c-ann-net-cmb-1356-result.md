A CMB announced after the decision instant is not readable at that lead (finding #1301: one announced and settled inside the lead window is not counted). Everything else is (s3)'s and the SRF cap is (s3c)'s. The references (s3c-ann-net) and (s3c), and the benchmarks B1, B1' and B2, are scored in the same run.
