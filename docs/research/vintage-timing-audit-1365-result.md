# Vintage and release-timing audit of every input (s3c-ann-net) reads (#1365)

**Hypothesis and step:** H5 (primary goal), registration design (information set). **Development, not a verdict.** An audit of declarations and publication clocks against primary sources. It scores nothing, reads no outcome and no day after 2026-09-03, fixes nothing in place and changes no declaration, record or candidate. Her ruling of 2 November 2026 (relayed on #1365) asked for it after an outside publication-readiness review.

Generated by `python3 scripts/vintage_timing_audit_1365.py build`; never hand-edited. The table is `docs/research/vintage-timing-audit-1365/audit.json`.

## What the candidate reads

(s3c-ann-net) is (s3) of the state-scaled calendar study, capped at the SRF line from full allotment, with its two settlement terms read from the auction announcement and sized by net settlement, decided at 18:00 America/New_York. Its terms are month_end, quarter_end, tax_date, settlement_coupon, settlement_bill, dom_19_23, post_month_end_window; the two settlement terms are sized (sized = True), it carries no extra term, it fits on direct pairs, and the scarcity state is held at its maximum, so no state value reaches a forecast. The terms of the calendar module it does not use are fomc_day, iorb_window. `tests/test_vintage_timing_audit_1365.py` pins that inventory and fails when a module of the chain names a panel column outside the audited set.

Not read, and so not audited: TGCR, BGCR, SOFR percentiles and volume, EFFR and DFF, the Treasury bill rates, the FR 2004 dealer position, the TGA, money-fund assets, the OFR repo series, SEC N-MFP, the DTCC GCF index, the Desk's SRF take-up.

### I1. SOFR: the observed leg of the spread, so the anchor, the level, the scale and every training pair

- **Source:** nyfed_sofr, SOFR (the NY Fed API, snapshot 20260906T195746Z)
- **Declared:** nyfed_sofr: 15:00 ET, 1 business_days after ref_date
- **Actual clock and primary source:** About 08:00 ET on the next business day. A same-day revision at about 14:30 ET, only if the rate moves by more than one basis point, and only on the day of first publication (https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/additional-information-about-reference-rates, read for this audit on 2 November 2026).
- **Holiday and exception rules:** No publication on days the NY Fed treats as holidays, and, with advance notice, on a day the Treasury repo market broadly treats as one (same page). I6 is that schedule.
- **Stored vintage:** Current vintage. A revision can only land at about 14:30 on the day of first publication, so the stored value is the value public at the 18:00 decision of that day.
- **Known revisions:** The API's revisionIndicator is blank on every row, so no revised date can be named; first prints for the past are not recoverable (#940). A revision is at least 2 bp (#1019).
- **Meaning against use:** The rate for the previous business day's transactions, dated by its effective date. At every decision the anchor is the previous panel row, at 18:00 and at 16:00 alike (checks.anchor).
- **Staleness (declaration later than needed):** none: the next SOFR is public at about 08:00 on the next business day.
- **Leakage risk (declaration earlier than evidenced):** none: 15:00 is after the only revision window and before 18:00.
