**The arm.** (s3c-gse) (#1274) reads a ramp on the calendar date: (d - 18) / 7 on day d from the 19th to the 25th, 0 otherwise. The agencies pay on the 25th, or on the next business day when the 25th is a weekend or a holiday, so in those months the ramp reads 0 on the payment day and peaks on the day before. **(s3c-gse-roll)** reads the 25th's value (7/7) on the payment day, the first business day on or after the 25th (`repo_model.data.market_holidays()` and weekends); the days before keep their own date's value, the 25th (which has no row in such a month) reads nothing, and the days after the payment day read 0. When the 25th is a business day the term is (s3c-gse)'s. The rule is declared and not tuned. It is read from the target row's own date and the holiday table alone, on direct pairs (`repo_model.s3c_gse_roll`). Every other term, the Huber fit, the ridge of 5 and the cap at the line from full allotment are (s3c)'s. **The control** is (s3c-gse), re-run unchanged in this run.
