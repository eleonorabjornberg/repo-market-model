**The arms.** (s3) of #1145 is #941's scaled mean reversion (a) with seven calendar terms in standardised units, a Huber fit and a ridge of 5 pseudo-days. The robust scale, the terms, the ridge of 5, direct pairs and the 21-day refit are (s3)'s. The benchmarks and the mean-reversion level read the pressure-day types of the month-end turn record in force since #1161, and B2 reads the thin-pool record in force since #1198, so (a), (s3) and B2 are not the figures of the #1145, #1172 and #1196 pages, which were scored on earlier readings (`--legacy-day-types` reproduces those); the arms are compared with each other here.
