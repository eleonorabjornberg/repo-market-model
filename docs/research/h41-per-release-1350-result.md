# The H.4.1 weeklies read per release at the 18:00 decision (#1350)

**Does reading the H.4.1 weeklies that feed the scarcity state per release (from the Thursday 16:30 print) instead of five calendar days after the Wednesday change the forecasts or their scores, at the 18:00 decision?** The answer is under the first heading below, from the intervals.

**Hypothesis and step:** H5 (primary goal), step 3; avenue #1338; her ruling of 1 November 2026 (chat, relayed; ledger item 99) on her open question 2 on PR #1315. **Development data, not a verdict** (scoring protocol v1, #640). **The registry's reading is not changed here** (`metadata/sources.json`, `docs/decisions/information-set.md`, every record and declaration are as they were), nothing is registered or published and no claim changes. Generated by `scripts/h41_per_release_1350.py page` from `docs/research/h41-per-release-1350/result.json`, not edited by hand; declared in `metadata/h41_per_release_1350.json` before any score.

**The two readings.** *Carried* (the registry in force): a Wednesday level is public five calendar days later at 16:30, so a decision reads it from the Monday. *Per release* (`metadata/h41_release_dating.json`, #541, through `repo_model.h41_release`, switched on for the length of a walk only): the level is public at its release's print, the Thursday after at 16:30 (the Board's moved date in a holiday week), so the 18:00 decision on that Thursday reads it. The two readings serve the same level from the Monday on, so they can differ only on Thursday and Friday decisions (and on the day a release was moved to, and on the Monday after a Wednesday that is a holiday with no panel row). Thursdays and Fridays are shown separately, with the Monday-to-Wednesday decisions beside them: on those both readings serve the same state, so their forecasts can differ there only through the refits, whose training pairs include earlier Thursday and Friday states.

**What reads the H.4.1.** The scarcity state has three legs, each read at its own latest public row: the reserve balances (the H.4.1's WRESBAL), the H.8 bank assets and ON RRP. Only the first is an H.4.1 weekly, so the per-release reading moves one leg of the state. The TGA is dated per release too in the variant registry, but no arm here reads it, so it is not tested. (s3c-ann-net) holds the state constant (`s3c_net.constant_states`) and reads no H.4.1 series: run as the ruling names it, it is expected to be unmoved, and the page says what it measured. The two arms that do read the state are (s3c+state), the scarcity-state arm of #1196 with (s3c)'s SRF cap, and (s3c-ann-net x state), built for this comparison: (s3c-ann-net)'s announced net settlement terms each times the as-of scarcity state (the sized arm of #1172 on the announced net). It is a measuring arm, not a proposal.

Panel digest `4ddc3882cd6d406b11e1088f8ff6195e16dde175a0ad6818abe40dd5bdac8999`. Decision instant 18:00 New York, pinned. Studentized stationary bootstrap (ruling 1), mean block 10 business days, 10,000 draws, seed 1350, two-sided 90% interval (the lower end is the one-sided 95% bound). Positive mean d favours the model named first. **A reading (gain, loss, undecided) is taken from the mean-d bound** (her ruling on #1009), with the skill interval beside it; for a Brier score the skill is the Brier skill score. **Caveat (Lens C, #958):** the lag-10 HAC standard error that studentizes the bound is understated about 2x after 2020, so an interval that excludes zero is narrower than it should be. The groups of target days keep the current method, which #1221 questions and #1228 will decide. A group in which two models score alike on every day is listed as identical and not tested; groups of fewer than 20 days are listed with their means and not tested.

**What kind of look this is.** The 2026 days were already seen by #845, by the #151 final test, and by #1229 and the studies after it. They are shown separately and decide nothing.
