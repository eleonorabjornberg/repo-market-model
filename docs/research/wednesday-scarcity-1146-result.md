## Reading

- The candidate's forecasts are identical under the two readings on every cell (the first table). With this candidate there is nothing to score prospectively under the Wednesday level, and a registration of it would register the same forecasts twice.
- The Wednesday level moves the state on a few dozen of the scored days and on none of the pressure onsets (reproducing #970). No state is called better here.
- Groups with fewer than 20 decision days carry counts and means only; their intervals are not computed.
- A figure here is paired and carries an interval where the group allows it, and is split by regime and pressure-day type; a pooled figure alone is not a result.

## Not checked

- **Never revised.** `WRBWFRBL` is the latest FRED vintage; its never-revised status is not re-verified. The check is a short run on Eleonora's Mac (`scripts/alfred_wednesday_1146.py`, pasted from the `needs-mac:` comment on the pull request).
- **A model that reads the state.** Neither #1145's arms nor any gradient-boosted model is run under the two readings; the candidate does not read the state.
- **Calendar first.** The candidate reads no calendar column, so its scores here are provisional under the calendar-first rule (`docs/research/provisional-calendar-register.md`); this page is not a calendar question and the rule changes none of the counts above.
