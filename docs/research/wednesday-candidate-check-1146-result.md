## Why: what it reads

(s3c-ann-net) is (s3) of the state-scaled calendar study with its two settlement terms sized by the announced net settlement, capped at the Standing Repo Facility line from full allotment (`repo_model.s3c_announced.ANN_NET_ARM`, `repo_model.s3c_candidate`). Its scale and centre come from the spread series. Its calendar terms are read from the calendar, the announcements and the policy register. The arm is a sized arm, and the sized arms' scarcity state is held at its maximum (`repo_model.s3c_net.constant_states`), so the state level is 1 on every day under either reading: the Wednesday level cannot reach the fit or the forecast through the state. The scarcity state exists in the arms that interact it with a term ((s2) and (s3+state) of #1145); this candidate is not one of them, which is the page of #1314 noting that "the model's own state is held at its maximum". The cap reads the policy register, not reserves.
