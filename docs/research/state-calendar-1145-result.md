**Why.** #1115 estimated one effect in bp per calendar term and applied it everywhere, so it lost in 2020 and 2021-23, where the state is quiet, and won in 2024 and 2025-26. The arms, all on #941's variant (a) unchanged and all reading the calendar at the target day on direct pairs: **(s1)** each term is estimated in the model's standardised units, so its effect in bp is the term times the anchor's as-of scale; **(s2)** (s1), and each term is also interacted with the as-of scarcity state (the own-leg state of #586 over 3: 0 on the floor, 1 when scarce), the coupon and bill settlements being two terms; **(s3)** (s1) with a Huber fit and a heavier ridge, so an outlier day does not set the effect and a term with few flagged days is shrunk toward 0; **(s4)** (s1) with the FOMC and IORB terms, the separate arm. The terms are #1115's, minus FOMC and IORB in (s1) to (s3): #1115 ran no per-term ablation, so no other term was shown to hurt on every reading, and none is dropped.
