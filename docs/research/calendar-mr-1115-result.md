## What this is not

Development data from 2018 to 2025, split by regime and target-day type, never a verdict. The arms read the calendar at the target day on direct pairs, so their class is not provisional (`docs/research/provisional-calendar-register.md`), but the estimation choices (the ridge, the three-flag minimum, the scale floor, the three-row IORB window) are this study's own, were not tuned, and are open to Eleonora's review. #941's variant was itself chosen after seeing 2018-2025.
