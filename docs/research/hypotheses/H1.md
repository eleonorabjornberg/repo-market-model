**Owner.** `eleonorabjornberg/repo-market-model`.

**Question.** Does the scoring module rank forecasts of known quality correctly, and is each benchmark computed as specified?

**Steps**
1. Write the synthetic suite before writing the scorer. It contains:
   - perfect, persistence and climatology forecasts;
   - shifted forecasts (bias of ±1, 2 and 5 bp);
   - under- and over-dispersed forecasts (×0.5, ×2);
   - lead-misaligned forecasts;
   - noisy truth at five graded skill levels.
2. The scorer must recover the known order on every pair, and its intervals must cover the true difference at their nominal rate. That rate is checked by simulation on resampled real series.
3. The benchmarks are reproduced by hand on 20 sampled days.

The old +5 bp judge's validation (#636) is folded in only if Eleonora keeps that judge for anything.

**Milestones**
- M1. Synthetic suite committed, with the expected order stated.
- M2. Scorer passes every check.
- M3. Interval coverage verified.
- M4. Auditor confirms.

**Markers.** Checks passed out of the total.

**Kill or fix.** Any failed check blocks every registration until fixed. The fix is logged under the protocol's change control.
