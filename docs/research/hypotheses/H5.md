# H5: PRIMARY GOAL. The SOFR − IORB spread distribution in bp

Draft by the orchestrating session, 12 October 2026, for Eleonora's review; ordered by her the same day. Plan: [`../plan.md`](../plan.md). Log: [`../log.md`](../log.md). Scoring follows the protocol of #640 once she decides it; sections that depend on it are pending #640.

**Owner.** `eleonorabjornberg/repo-market-model`.

**Question.** Does a registered distribution forecast of SOFR − IORB (h = 1–5) beat as-of persistence and calendar climatology on CRPS by at least the MME, on prospective days?

**Groundwork**
- G1. Protocol decided (#640).
- G2. One scoring module and benchmarks B1–B3, validated on synthetic forecasts (H1).
- G3. Prospective logging for candidates and benchmarks, extending the live record.
- G4. Power study: how many days detect the MME.
- G5. The construction fixes merged (#623).

**Development steps**
1. Re-score the phase 2 models (gbm, cross-conformal, v1 funding declaration) under the protocol on development days. This sets the baseline, measured against the B1 that widens with horizon (#634, part a).
2. Add the post-phase-2 inputs one family at a time, with an ablation:
   - settlement and H.4.1 timing;
   - the IORB change;
   - the scarcity state read per leg;
   - satiation distance;
   - tail spread.

   Directives: #634 and #632.
3. Diagnose the known weaknesses, each as its own logged sub-question:
   - 2021–23, where every gbm loses to persistence;
   - the upper tail, judged by twCRPS above 0 bp.
4. Use H2's upper-bound result (#635) to decide whether more model work is worthwhile. If the ceiling is low, stop at the best available and register it.
5. Choose the primary candidate from development by the protocol score alone, and record the variant count.
6. Rewrite the model-risk validation report for the chosen candidate (Eleonora, 13 October 2026, plan amendment
   #717). It is a rewrite of the existing [`docs/model/validation.md`](../../model/validation.md), not a new page.
   - It keeps the model-risk structure ruled on #119: purpose and use, data, methodology, assumptions and
     limitations, performance by regime, outcomes analysis, monitoring.
   - It uses development evidence only and carries a use-limitation statement.
   - The rewrite replaces the phase 2 content. The phase 2 version stays in git history, and its records stay
     untouched in `docs/runs/`. Until this step, `docs/model/validation.md` stays as it is.
   - The step updates `scripts/validation_report.py` and its tests as the rewrite needs.
   - Every figure in the report sits in a generated block written from a record by `scripts/emit_results.py` or
     `scripts/validation_report.py`; the prose states none.
   - The rewrite goes to Eleonora together with the registration, so she reads it before she attests the
     registration.

**Registration and confirmation.** Register one primary, plus up to two secondaries. Then 60 / 125 / 250 prospective days.
At each checkpoint the report's outcomes-analysis and monitoring sections are updated with that checkpoint's verdict.

**Milestones (done when)**
- M1. G1–G5 complete: protocol merged, scorer validated, logging live, power study in, #623 merged.
- M2. Baseline re-score published on development days.
- M3. Ablation of the new inputs published (which families help, with intervals).
- M4. 2021–23 and upper-tail diagnoses published.
- M5. Candidate registered (attested).

  registration. It precedes M5 in time; the number is kept so that M5-M8 are not renumbered.
- M6, M7, M8. Checkpoints at 60, 125 and 250 days, each with a verdict or "continue", and each with the report's
  outcomes analysis updated.

**Progress markers**
- Development CRPS skill against B1 and B2 (pooled, 2021–23, upper tail), for the best candidate so far.
- Variant count.
- Prospective days logged.
- Running skill with its interval (shown as "not yet decision-grade" before 60 days).

**Kill.** By the protocol's futility rule. In development, by H2's verdict "data problem", which moves effort to H3 and H4.
