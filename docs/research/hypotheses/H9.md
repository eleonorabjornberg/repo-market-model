# H9: SECONDARY GOAL. Richer targets

Draft by the orchestrating session, 12 October 2026, for Eleonora's review; ordered by her the same day. Plan: [`../plan.md`](../plan.md). Log: [`../log.md`](../log.md). Scoring follows the protocol of #640 once she decides it; sections that depend on it are pending #640.

**Owner.** `eleonorabjornberg/repo-market-model`.

**Question.** Do forecasts of richer rate series beat their own persistence and climatology? And does any of them improve the H5 forecast when used as an input? The series are:
- the SOFR 99th percentile minus IORB, and SOFR dispersion;
- TGCR, BGCR and EFFR, each minus IORB;
- ON RRP take-up;
- standing repo facility take-up.

**Groundwork**
- G1–G3 as for H5.
- Each target sourced with an established publication instant (#629, #630).
- Each target's own B1 and B2 built into the scorer.

**Development steps**
1. Screen forecastability and lead against pressure episodes (#639, step 1).
2. Pick at most three targets by the screen. Fit H5's model class to each (#639, step 2).
3. Nested development test: H5's best candidate with and without each target's forecast as an input.

**Registration and confirmation.** One registration per target (Holm across targets), plus one nested registration against H5.

**The Holm family: near-identical targets count as one member** (Eleonora's ruling of 21 October 2026 on #971; applied by #1445; the matching sentence for the scoring protocol is drafted under its section 8 as draft amendment 5, for her review, and binds only when she merges it).
- **The rule.** Targets that are near-identical count as **one** member of the Holm family, so that copies of one series do not dilute the correction as separate tests. A family of k members has k Holm thresholds (0.05/k for the first), and a pooled group is one of the k.
- **Series pooled under it today: none.** The family is the at most three targets that step 2's declared rule chooses (`metadata/richer_targets_706.json`, `choice.rule`): {`bgcr_dispersion_bps`, `on_rrp_change_bn`, `sofr_dispersion_bps`}. TGCR − IORB and BGCR − IORB are not in it, and are not pooled into it: step 2 (#706) dropped them before the family was formed, by "drop a target whose pooled rank correlation with SOFR − IORB (level, all scored days) has magnitude 0.8 or more". They move with SOFR − IORB at 0.995 in daily changes (Lens C, #958), and SOFR − IORB is H5's target, not an H9 one. Only step 3 (#842) has used Holm across the family.
- **How a future target is judged near-identical.** By the same measure and cut as step 2: the pooled rank correlation of its level with that of an existing family member, over all scored development days (no locked day, no confirmation day), has magnitude 0.8 or more. A target that meets it against a member is pooled with that member and does not add a test; a target that meets it against SOFR − IORB alone is dropped, as step 2 drops it. The correlation, its days and the member it is pooled with are stated in the registration. This is the only definition proposed here; a different measure or cut is her ruling.
- **Open, for her (marked questions on the pull request of #1445).** (1) The present pair BGCR dispersion and SOFR dispersion has a level Spearman of 0.84 (change Pearson 0.64, #971 correction), which is above 0.8 on the pooling measure. This note leaves them as two members, as the issue says, because the 0.8 cut was declared against SOFR − IORB, not between members. Default: two members. (2) How a pooled group yields one p-value for Holm is not ruled. Default: the member that ranked highest under step 2's rule represents the group.
