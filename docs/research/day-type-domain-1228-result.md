## The two intervals

**Current.** The group's own days go to `scoring_protocol.paired_tests` (`run_pairs`): the days are sorted into one short series, blocks of mean length 10 are drawn over its positions, and the mean difference is studentized by a lag-10 HAC standard error on that short series. For quarter-ends, whose days are about three months apart, one block joins days years apart.

**Domain.** The whole aligned target-day series of `d = benchmark - candidate` is resampled with the same stationary bootstrap, block, seed and draws as the pooled test (`domain_intervals.domain_estimates`); the statistic is the mean of `d` over the resampled days that belong to the group; the interval is the 90% percentile interval of that statistic. The point estimate is the group mean in both. A resample that draws no day of a group would make the row unavailable, with the reason; none did here unless a row below says `undefined`.

**Two things differ at once.** The domain interval is a percentile interval and is not studentized, because no module here studentizes a domain statistic (it would need a HAC standard error of the group's mean over the resample). So a change below is the resampling frame and the studentization together; this page does not separate them. A reading is taken from the bound on mean d for both (gain: lower end above 0; loss: upper end below 0), her #1009 ruling. Lens C's caveat (#958) applies to the current interval and is not removed by the domain one.

## What this shows and does not show

Development data on 2018-2025 for a choice of interval, not a verdict and not a result about the model. It measures how much the choice of interval moves the day-type readings of #1196; it does not say which interval is right (the simulation in `tests/test_domain_intervals_1228.py` measures coverage on an AR(1) series with group days 60 apart, beside the current one). Every row, not only the changed ones, is in `result.json`. Not done: a studentized domain statistic; the same measurement on the other pages built on `run_pairs` (#1182 and about twenty more); the regime rows (they are contiguous); the minimum group size (the floor stays 20 for reporting; groups under 60 days are labelled thin in the follow-up, her ruling).
