## Availability: are the decision day's results public before 18:00?

**Both sources are used.** A date's figure is public at the decision instant of its own day when the latest lastUpdated of its records is at or before 18:00 New York on that day; a date written later (or rewritten later) is withheld and reads as no take-up.
