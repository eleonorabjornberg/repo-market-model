## The source and what it can say

The series are the New York Fed's `PDSIRRA-UTSETTOT`, `PDSIRRA-UTSTTOT`, `PDSIOSB-UTSETTOT`, `PDSIOSB-UTSTTOT` (USD millions), snapshotted under `tests/fixtures/snapshots/nyfed-pd-financing/` with their SHA-256 and retrieval instants in `manifest.json`. They run weekly (Wednesdays) from 2013-04-03 to 2026-10-14; 542 of 707 weeks have a level, because the New York Fed withholds ('*') some figures for confidentiality (weeks withheld by series: `PDSIOSB-UTSETTOT` 0, `PDSIOSB-UTSTTOT` 69, `PDSIRRA-UTSETTOT` 94, `PDSIRRA-UTSTTOT` 13). A week with any of the four withheld has no level; the level read is that of the latest released week that has one, carried over the withheld weeks (how many is reported below), and the term is 0 on a decision day with no state (fewer than 13 defined weeks in the year before the level read). **Release.** The New York Fed's page says: "Data are updated on Thursdays at approximately 4:15 p.m. with the previous week's statistics." The declared instant is 6 market business days after the as-of Wednesday at 16:30 America/New_York, so a decision at 16:00 on the release Thursday reads the week before and the next business day's reads the new one (`tests/test_s3c_pd.py`). The page says "approximately" and no release archive was read. **Vintage.** The snapshot is the latest revised vintage the API served, not first prints. **Not the OFR route.** The Office of Financial Research chart the directive names draws on these series, but the OFR's data API serves the reverse-repo leg only to 2021-12-29, so the New York Fed's own API is used for both legs.

**What the share of defined days means.** A state exists on a decision day only when at least 13 of the 52 weeks before the level read have a level. The tables below count the decision days that have one; on the others (s3c-pd) differs from (s3c) only through the refit.
