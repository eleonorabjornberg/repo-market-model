## The three intervals

**Current.** The group's own days go to `scoring_protocol.paired_tests` (`run_pairs`): the days are sorted into one short series, blocks of mean length 10 are drawn over its positions, and the mean difference is studentized by a lag-10 HAC standard error on that short series. For quarter-ends, whose days are about three months apart, one block joins days years apart.

**Percentile domain** (#1228). The whole aligned target-day series of `d = benchmark - candidate` is resampled with the same stationary bootstrap, block, seed and draws as the pooled test; the statistic is the mean of `d` over the resampled days that belong to the group; the interval is the 90% percentile interval of that statistic. Not studentized.

**Studentized domain** (#1266). The same resamples and the same statistic. Each resample's group mean is divided by its own HAC standard error: with `I_t` the indicator that the day at position `t` of the resampled sequence is in the group, `K` the number of such days and `m` their mean, the series `u_t = I_t (d_t - m)` over all `n` positions has the Newey-West (Bartlett, lag 10) long-run variance `V`, and the standard error is `sqrt(V / n) * n / K`. The bounds are the group mean less the 95th and 5th percentiles of those t-draws times the same standard error on the original series, as the pooled rows are read. For a group of every day it is the pooled test's interval to rounding (pinned in `tests/test_studentized_domain_1266.py`). A resample that draws no day of a group would make the row unavailable, with the reason. A resample with no positive long-run variance (it drew too few group days to vary) is left out and counted, as #1239 does for the pooled test; the row says how many.

The point estimate is the group mean in all three. A reading is taken from the bound on mean d for each (gain: lower end above 0; loss: upper end below 0), her #1009 ruling. Lens C's caveat (#958) applies to the current interval and is not removed by the domain ones.
