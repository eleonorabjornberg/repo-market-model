**Which change explains which.** A stage is the merge of one pull request together with whatever main took between it and the stage before (the `merged since` column). The change a row moved at is the one named there: the as-of order of a group's fields is #1060, the B2 thin-pool record in force is #1198, and the no-variance resamples dropped from the mean-d studentization (with their count) is #1239; the stage named for #1215 is the tree on which the committed file is reproduced, so a row that moved there would be #1215's reporting change. Where a stage carries other changes that can reach the scorer, a move at that stage is not attributed to one of them. A change that moved a row at no stage listed, or at the last stage without the dropped-resample count beside it, is not attributed.
