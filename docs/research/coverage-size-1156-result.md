# Size of the coverage requirement under three hit rules (#1156)

**Hypothesis and step:** H1 (scoring protocol synthetic suite) and H5 step 5 (candidate choice). **Development, synthetic data, not a verdict.** Follow-up to PR #1137 (#1130) and Eleonora's ruling of 22 October 2026 there (answers 1, 2, 3 and 5 at their defaults; answer 4, the hit rule, left to this measurement). Generated by `scripts/coverage_size_1156.py page` from `docs/research/coverage-size-1156/result.json`, not edited by hand; declared in `metadata/coverage_size_1156.json` before any figure.

**What was measured.** Calibrated forecasters of a whole-bp target with mass points (an AR(1) latent rounded to whole bp, three regimes of unequal length, 2,000 days, leads 1 to 5; the forecaster knows the law, so its integer quantiles tie the outcomes). Kupiec and independence on every h-th day at lead h, at q05/q95 and q01/q99 by lead and regime; Holm over the family of one hit rule's tests at 5% (`enforce_coverage_requirement(..., family="holm", alpha=0.05)`, with the flag patched on inside the script only: in the tree `COVERAGE_REQUIREMENT_IN_FORCE` is False). A path *fails* when Holm rejects anywhere in the family. 2,000 paths; intervals are Wilson 95% intervals of the share of paths.

## What this shows and does not show

- It shows the size of the design on a synthetic world built to be calibrated, with whole-bp mass points. It is not a result about any candidate, benchmark or published record, and no verdict.
- The forecaster's quantiles are integers, so ties are frequent; a forecaster with continuous quantiles has no ties and is a different case. The world's regimes are three blocks the declaration fixes, standing for the protocol's development split (answer 5); the labels' definition is hers.
- The requirement is not in force (`COVERAGE_REQUIREMENT_IN_FORCE` stays False in the tree), no attestation entry is added and no published record changes. Setting the flag is a later step after her merge of the record and her own `GO`.
- Not checked: HAC (`method="hac"`) size; other forecasters (a pooled-calibrated or a regime-miscalibrated one); the real candidates, which need walks on her Mac.
