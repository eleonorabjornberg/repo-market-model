**How the rows are read.** Every paired row shows the skill interval and the interval on mean d. By Eleonora's ruling on #1009 a row is read on the studentized bound on mean d: **gain** when the lower bound is above zero, **loss** when the upper bound is below zero, **undecided** otherwise. The reading on the skill interval is shown beside it, because the two intervals are built from different studentized draws and can fall on different sides of zero.

**Caveat (Lens C of the five-lens review, #958).** The lag-10 HAC standard error that studentizes the mean-d bound is understated about 2× after 2020 (about 2.3× in 2021–23, 1.7× in 2024–26), and the bootstrap block of 10 days is an order of magnitude shorter than the autocorrelation of d. The bound is therefore optimistic: an interval that excludes zero is narrower than it should be after 2020. A reading on this page is evidence for a question, not a verdict.
