**What changed, and what the directive's wording leaves open.** (s3c) carries `settlement_coupon` and `settlement_bill` as 0/1 flags (1 when the gross settlement of the target day is above zero), not as sizes. (s3c-net) replaces the two by the signed NET settlement of the target day in units of USD 100 bn: the offerings less the announced publicly held maturing amount, the bill types and the coupon types separately (`repo_model.net_settlement`, #426; the SOMA leg cancels), read at the 16:00 decision of the day before at lead 1, the only lead at which the gross flags are read; the sign is kept. Everything else is (s3)'s (the other terms, the Huber fit, the ridge of 5, direct pairs) and the SRF cap is (s3c)'s. That makes two changes in each term at once, flag to size and gross to net. **The control (s3c-gross)** takes the first alone: the same signed sizes in USD 100 bn from the panel's gross settlement. Net against the control isolates gross against net; the control against (s3c) isolates flag against size. The control is a reference, not an arm.
