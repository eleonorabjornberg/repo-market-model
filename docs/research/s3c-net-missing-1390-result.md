# The net settlement reader's missing state, and (s3c-ann-net) with the missing days read two ways (#1390)

**Does a net settlement figure of 0.0 mean zero, and does reading the missing ones another way move the candidate's score?** On the development window, the drop reading's pooled grid CRPS against (s3c-ann-net): +0.1% [+0.0%, +0.2%]; d +0.002 [+0.000, +0.004]: **gain**; on the 2026 development days, the drop reading's pooled grid CRPS against (s3c-ann-net): +0.1% [-0.3%, +0.4%]; d +0.002 [-0.004, +0.006]: **undecided**.

**Hypothesis and step:** H5 (primary goal), registration design (data meaning), step 3 on the candidate (s3c-ann-net); her ruling of 2 November 2026 (chat, relayed on #1390: the second outside review). **Development data, not a verdict** (scoring protocol v1, #640). **(s3c-ann-net) stays the candidate of avenue #1318 and (s3c) the H5 candidate; nothing here changes either, and what a missing amount means is hers.** Nothing is registered or published, no figure of a published record changes, and the published declaration `metadata/net_settlement.json` does not change. Generated by `scripts/s3c_net_missing_1390.py page` from `docs/research/s3c-net-missing-1390/result.json`, not edited by hand; declared in `metadata/s3c_net_missing_1390.json` before any score.

**What the reader does.** For each settlement day and each class (bill types, coupon types) `net_settlement.net_state` now names the state: `complete` (fully announced, with a maturing estimate), `absent` (nothing of the class settles), `missing_maturing` (fully announced, no announced record carries a maturing estimate, so the net reads the maturing amount as 0.0 and the class drains its whole offering), `incomplete` (partly announced: the class is left out and reads 0.0, #679) and `unannounced` (on the schedule, nothing announced: reads 0.0). `net_for_day` returns the same numbers as before (tests pin hand values that the function on main also returns). A cash management bill (CMB) announced after the instant is on no published schedule (#738), so nothing at the instant can say it is coming; it is counted below in hindsight and is not part of the state, the flag or the per-forecast log.

## Step 3: the candidate with the missing days read two ways

**Two readings, stated before any score.** *As now*: (s3c-ann-net), the missing maturing amount read as 0.0, a class not fully announced left out, the state logged and not read. *Drop*: a class whose state is `missing_maturing`, `incomplete` or `unannounced` contributes 0.0 to the net term, and a 0/1 flag `net_missing` (1 when either class of the target day rests on a missing figure at the decision instant, 18:00 of D = T - h) enters beside the two net terms. Only `missing_maturing` differs in the net between the two (the other two states read 0.0 in both); the flag is what is new. Both on direct pairs, with the SRF cap of (s3c). The references (s3c-ann-net) and (s3c), and the benchmarks B1, B1' and B2, are scored in the same run.

Panel digest `4ddc3882cd6d406b11e1088f8ff6195e16dde175a0ad6818abe40dd5bdac8999`. Studentized stationary bootstrap (ruling 1), mean block 10 business days, 10,000 draws, seed 1390, two-sided 90% interval (the lower end is the one-sided 95% bound). Positive mean d favours the model named first. **A reading (gain, loss, undecided) is taken from the mean-d bound** (her ruling on #1009), with the skill interval beside it; for a Brier score the skill is the Brier skill score. **Caveat (Lens C, #958):** the lag-10 HAC standard error that studentizes the bound is understated about 2x after 2020, so an interval that excludes zero is narrower than it should be. The intervals of a group of target days keep the **current method** (#1221, #1228). Where two models differ on few days of a group the bound may not be computed: the unstudentized interval of mean d is then given beside it, and no reading is taken from that. Groups of fewer than 20 days are listed and not tested. The benchmarks are tested on the pooled, lead, regime and pressure-day-type groups only.

**What kind of look this is.** The 2026 days were already seen by #845, by the #151 final test, and by #1229 and the studies after it. They are shown separately and decide nothing.
