## What the candidate already reads (step 1)

The step asks first whether (s3c-ann-net) already reads R or S, because if it read both, H7f would be answered by construction. It reads neither, established from the arm and its state series, not assumed:

- **S: no.** The candidate is the sized arm of #1172 with its scarcity state held at its maximum (`s3c_net.constant_states`): on all 2,104 panel days the level it reads is 1, so its sized settlement terms are the plain signed net over USD 100 bn. The 'scarcity state read per leg' row of #1483 was a different contrast (every term times the state, an interaction); S as an additive input has not been measured on it.
- **R: no.** Its terms are the calendar and settlement terms (`month_end`, `quarter_end`, `tax_date`, `settlement_coupon`, `settlement_bill`, `dom_19_23`, `post_month_end_window`), its scale is the median absolute daily change of the spread (spread_mr_fhs, #941), and its cap reads the SRF line from the policy register (s3c_candidate); no reserves series enters.

**The arm adds what is missing, both:** `rs_ratio` (R in percentage points of reserves over the H.8 total assets of all commercial banks) and `rs_state` (S over 3), as two extra terms of the Huber fit after the candidate's terms. Everything else is the candidate's. The contrast therefore cannot separate R from S: a gain is R+S together, and a loss or an undecided reading says R+S as read here adds nothing to the candidate.
