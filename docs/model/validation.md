## 1. Model purpose and use

The model forecasts the distribution of the daily spread between SOFR and the interest rate on reserve balances, in
basis points, one to five business days ahead. It is H5's candidate: the model the development steps of the research
plan chose, by the protocol score alone, to put forward for registration.

It is built for readers who want to see, with every claim paired against a benchmark and split by regime and by type of
day, what a point-in-time forecast of repo-market pressure can and cannot do. It is not a basis for any decision that
carries risk.

<!-- generated: validation-use -->
<!-- Generated by scripts/emit_results.py (scripts/validation_report.py) from docs/runs/, metadata/ and docs/model/. Do not edit by hand. -->

**What it forecasts.** The candidate is `gbm_funding`. It forecasts the distribution of the SOFR − IORB spread in basis points, as 23 quantiles of the spread on each of the next 1 to 5 business days after the decision day. The primary score is grid CRPS over h = 1 to 5 (scoring protocol v1, `docs/decisions/scoring-protocol.md`).

**Hypothesis.** H5, the primary goal of the project (`docs/research/hypotheses/H5.md`): a registered distribution forecast of the spread beats as-of persistence and calendar climatology on this score by at least the minimum margin of the protocol, on prospective days. This report is H5's step 6.

**What it must not be used for.** It is not a stress-warning system and not a basis for VaR, limits, liquidity or capital, or desk sizing: "warns of stress" is never claimed. Its users are researchers and readers of the development pages; it carries no decision authority.

## 3. Methodology and controls

**The model.** What the candidate is, what it reads, and how it is fitted, from the declaration of the measurement that
scored it. Every choice was declared and committed before any score, and development days give a score and an interval,
never a verdict.

<!-- generated: validation-candidate -->
<!-- Generated by scripts/emit_results.py (scripts/validation_report.py) from docs/runs/, metadata/ and docs/model/. Do not edit by hand. -->

**The model.** `gbm_funding`: ml.fit_gradient_boosted_quantiles at the 23 levels on the published features only, uncalibrated: the phase 2 gbm re-scored under the protocol, and the ablation parent of (b)

**Inputs.** The published funding declaration's features: `reserve_balances`, `sofr_p25`, `sofr_p75`, `sofr_volume`, `spread_bps`, `tbill_13w`, `tbill_4w`, `tga`, `treasury_settlement`. the published funding declaration's features (scripts/live_record.py CRPS_FEATURES); treasury_settlement is dropped at h >= 2 when read as published (published_features), kept at every lead when announcement-dated.

**Levels and horizons.** The protocol's grid of 23 levels, at horizons 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 business days, read at each lead by the as-of rule (`asof.InformationRule(horizon=h)`).

**Fitting.** A walk-forward fit, minimum history 61 scored days, refitted every 21 scored days, decision time 16:00. A decision day is scored when every one of its target days is on or before 2025-12-31 and has a published outcome.

**Status.** Uncalibrated: no recalibration layer corrects its quantiles. Not registered: it is a candidate of the development steps of H5 and no confirmation day has scored it.

**Declaration.** `metadata/spread_distribution_634.json`, declared by the pull request that closes #634 before any score was computed.

**The Treasury clearing mandate**, a limitation of the target and not of the model.

**The Treasury clearing mandate.** (Eleonora's scope of 2 October 2026 on #119, relayed by the orchestrating session.)

- **The dates.** The SEC's Treasury clearing rule requires central clearing of eligible secondary-market cash Treasury trades **by the end of 2026** and of eligible Treasury repo **by mid-2027** (SEC Release 34-102487: https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2025/34-102487.pdf; not fetched: this environment's network policy refused the host). The release itself was not read, so the timing rests on the scope; the New York Fed's speech, the New York Fed's speech of 9 July 2026 (https://www.newyorkfed.org/newsevents/speeches/2026/per260709) says it requires requires eligible secondary-market Treasury cash transactions to be centrally cleared "by the end of 2026" and eligible Treasury repo and reverse repo "by mid-2027", and that repo activity "has begun migrating from uncleared to cleared markets".
- **What it means for the target.** SOFR includes the trades used in BGCR (tri-party and GCF repo) plus transactions cleared through FICC's delivery-versus-payment repo service, so it does not cover repo that is neither tri-party, GCF nor FICC-cleared (the New York Fed's reference-rate methodology, https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/additional-information-about-reference-rates). Repo migrating into clearing therefore drifts SOFR's composition and volume. The migration is gradual and already under way: its footnote cites Office of Financial Research staff analysis that approximately 58 percent of repo volumes subject to the rule were already being cleared in the first eight months of 2025 (the scope of 2 October 2026 said early 2025).
- **The expected effect.** The expectation of little change to SOFR's median but fatter tails is the OFR's (the OFR's blog post of 22 April 2025: https://www.financialresearch.gov/the-ofr-blog/2025/04/22/how-the-treasury-clearing-rule-for-repo-might-affect-sofr/; not fetched: this environment's network policy refused the host). This project's target is the tail.
  The scope gives the exact compliance dates, the last day of 2026 for cash trades and the last day of the first half of the next year for repo. They are not written out here, because `tests/test_docs_freshness.py` refuses a published page that states a date that has not happened; the fetched speech's own wording is used instead.
- **Where it falls.** Both compliance dates fall after the panel ends (2026-09-03), so there is no clean break inside the scoring window or the near-blind tier. The drift overlaps the 2025 regime and the locked period: watch it when the lockbox is opened, above all for any input built on `SOFR_volume`.

**How it is scored** (the declaration `metadata/spread_distribution_634.json`, written before any score).

- **Score.** grid CRPS over h = 1 to 5; key secondary, upper-tail twCRPS, weight 1{y > 0 bp}.
- **Benchmarks.** B1, repo_model.protocol_benchmarks.b1_forecast on the published panel; B2, repo_model.protocol_benchmarks.b2_forecast on the published panel.
- **Pairing.** Each day's difference is the benchmark's score minus the candidate's, so a positive value favours the candidate: d = S(benchmark) - S(candidate); positive favours the candidate; skill = mean(d) / mean(S(benchmark)).
- **Interval.** stationary bootstrap, mean block length 10 business days, 10,000 draws, seed 634, one-sided 95% (the lower bound of mean(d); the upper bound of skill); Diebold-Mariano with Newey-West lag 10 beside it.
- **Regimes** (of the decision day, drawn after the fact): 2018-19, 2020, 2021-23, 2024, 2025-26.
- **Pressure-day types** (of the target day): quarter-end, month-end, tax-date, ordinary.
