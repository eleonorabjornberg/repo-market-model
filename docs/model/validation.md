## 1. Model purpose and use

The model forecasts the distribution of the daily spread between SOFR and the interest rate on reserve balances, in
basis points, one to five business days ahead. It is H5's candidate: the model the development steps of the research
plan chose, by the protocol score, and that Eleonora froze for registration.

It is built for readers who want to see, with every claim paired against a benchmark and split by regime and by type of
day, what a point-in-time forecast of repo-market pressure can and cannot do. It is not a basis for any decision that
carries risk.

<!-- generated: validation-use -->
<!-- Generated by scripts/emit_results.py (scripts/validation_report.py) from docs/runs/, metadata/ and docs/model/. Do not edit by hand. -->

**What it forecasts.** The candidate is (s3c-ann-net), H5's frozen candidate. It forecasts the distribution of the spread of SOFR minus the administered reserve rate (IOER before 29 July 2021, IORB after; written SOFR − IORB) in basis points, as 23 quantiles of the spread on each of the next 1 to 5 business days after the decision day. The primary score is grid CRPS (23 levels) averaged over leads h = 1..5 (scoring protocol v1, `docs/decisions/scoring-protocol.md`).

**Hypothesis.** H5, the primary goal of the project (`docs/research/hypotheses/H5.md`): a registered distribution forecast of the spread beats as-of persistence and calendar climatology on this score by at least the minimum margin of the protocol, on prospective days. This report is H5's step 6, rewritten for the frozen candidate.

**What it must not be used for.** It is not a stress-warning system and not a basis for VaR, limits, liquidity or capital, or desk sizing: "warns of stress" is never claimed. Its users are researchers and readers of the development pages; it carries no decision authority.

## 3. Methodology and controls

**The model.** What the candidate is, what it reads, and how it is fitted, from the declaration of the measurement that
scored it. Every choice was declared and committed before any score, and development days give a score and an interval,
never a verdict.

<!-- generated: validation-candidate -->

**The model.** (s3c-ann-net): (s3c), which is (s3) of the state-scaled calendar study (the scaled mean reversion with calendar terms read at the target day in standardised units, fitted with a Huber estimator and a heavier ridge) capped at the Standing Repo Facility line from full allotment onwards with a margin of zero, with its two settlement terms read from the auction announcement on the days before settlement and sized by net settlement

**What it is, as the declaration states it.** (s3c-ann), sized by #1273's net settlement: the two settlement terms enter as the signed net of the target day in units of USD 100 bn (repo_model.s3c_net.NET_ARM's form, sized = True with the scarcity state held at its maximum), the net of target day T as announced at the 16:00 decision instant of D = T - h, at every lead h = 1..5. A class announced only in part reads 0 until fully announced (repo_model.net_settlement, #679), and a cash management bill counts only from its own announcement (#738)

**The cap.** Every quantile clipped at min(q, line + margin) on target days at full allotment (the standing_repo entry in force on the target day, public at the decision instant, states no aggregate operation limit); (s3) itself on every other day (repo_model.srf_line (#1241); declared in `metadata/s3c_candidate_1255.json`).

**The sizing of the settlement terms.** Net settlement: the signed net of the target day (offering less maturing, as announced at the decision instant of the decision day, 18:00 America/New_York since #1312) in units of USD 100 bn, at every lead h = 1..5 (`repo_model.s3c_announced (ANN_NET_ARM, AnnouncedNetSizeTable)`; declared in `metadata/s3c_announced_settlement_1288.json`).

**Inputs.** The target's own history and the calendar columns at the target day, on direct pairs (`training_pairs="direct"`); the two settlement terms from the auction announcement, public from 12:00 America/New_York on announcemt_date (docs/decisions/information-set.md, rule 2 (#541's wording, #1299); metadata/sources.json, treasury_auctions, scheduled_availability (#1310)); the SRF line and the standing_repo entry in force, as public at the decision instant. Nothing it reads is later than the decision instant (`docs/decisions/information-set.md`).

**Levels and horizons.** The protocol's grid of 23 levels, at horizons 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 business days.

**Fitting.** A walk-forward fit; the declaration states the walk as "(s3) is #1145's walk (refit every 21 decision days, direct pairs)". The decision instant is 18:00 America/New_York. The development window is the decision days whose five targets are on or before 2025-12-31; the 2026 days are shown apart and decide nothing.

**Status.** Not recalibrated: no recalibration layer corrects its quantiles. Frozen as the H5 candidate (development, not a verdict, 2026-10-29: https://github.com/eleonorabjornberg/repo-market-model/issues/1310) and not registered: no confirmation day has scored it.

**Declaration.** `metadata/s3c_announced_settlement_1288.json`, declared by the pull request that closes #1288 before any score was computed; the candidate's record is `docs/research/h5-candidate.json`.

**The Treasury clearing mandate.** (Eleonora's scope of 2 October 2026 on #119, relayed by the orchestrating session.)

- **The dates.** The SEC's Treasury clearing rule requires central clearing of eligible secondary-market cash Treasury trades **by the end of 2026** and of eligible Treasury repo **by mid-2027** (SEC Release 34-102487: https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2025/34-102487.pdf; not fetched: this environment's network policy refused the host). - **What it means for the target.** SOFR includes the trades used in BGCR (tri-party and GCF repo) plus transactions cleared through FICC's delivery-versus-payment repo service, so it does not cover repo that is neither tri-party, GCF nor FICC-cleared (the New York Fed's reference-rate methodology, https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/additional-information-about-reference-rates). Repo migrating into clearing therefore drifts SOFR's composition and volume. - **The expected effect.** The expectation of little change to SOFR's median but fatter tails is the OFR's (the OFR's blog post of 22 April 2025: https://www.financialresearch.gov/the-ofr-blog/2025/04/22/how-the-treasury-clearing-rule-for-repo-might-affect-sofr/; not fetched: this environment's network policy refused the host).
