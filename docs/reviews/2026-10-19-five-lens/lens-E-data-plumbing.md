## 1. Executive summary (top 3)

1. **The DTCC GCF Repo Index is a same-day public Treasury GC rate, published about 15:30 ET, i.e. inside the 16:00 decision window of the day it describes.** It is the only public repo *rate* for day T−1 that exists at the T−1 16:00 decision; SOFR(T−1) is not out until T 08:00. The repo blocked the DTCC source in the #630 due diligence because "no publication instant can be established from the pages" (`docs/pivot/new-sources-due-diligence.md:33`); DTCC's own fact sheet states the instant (verified, §4.3). The repo's existing copy of the GCF rate (OFR `REPO-GCF_AR_OO-P`) is suppressed on 1,847 of 2,102 days (verified from the tracked snapshot), so the DTCC index is not redundant. A scratch check on the 144 days where the OFR value exists: a same-day GCF read cuts the lead-1 error against as-of persistence from 9.95 to 6.52 bp on the 20 days the spread moved ≥5 bp, and 7.47→6.17 bp on pressure target days, with no gain on calm days (verified, computed, caveats in §5). **Expected value for H5: high at lead 1, tail-weighted.**
2. **The OFR cleared-repo series are declared two business days late; the OFR states a one-day lag with a 3 p.m. release.** `metadata/sources.json:635-643` and `metadata/sources_measurement.json:64-67` declare DVP and GCF at 16:00 two business days after the reference date. The OFR says "The preliminary release is published daily at 3 p.m." and "Data for centrally cleared repo markets are generally made available with a one-day lag" (tri-party: two days). So DVP(T−2) and GCF(T−2) are readable at the T−1 16:00 decision, the same date as SOFR(T−2). The H3 segment screen was run a day staler than SOFR; re-declaring is a one-line change in the measurement registry and a cheap re-screen. **Expected value: medium (a corrected condition on a prior "no gain").**
3. **The reserves and TGA legs read H.4.1 *week averages* (WRESBAL, WTREGEN), while the decision record and `asof.py` describe them as Wednesday levels; the Wednesday levels exist on FRED as WRBWFRBL and WDTGAL.** A week average is centred three and a half days before the Wednesday and smooths the intra-week drain; a strategist reads the Wednesday level (or the daily DTS for the TGA). The docs mismatch is tracked (#485 item 5); the data choice is not. **Expected value: low-to-medium for H5 (feeds the scarcity state, not the daily signal); cost one day.**

Everything else I checked in `metadata/sources.json` is declared on the conservative side (later than the truth) or exactly; I found **no optimistic availability declaration** and **no off-by-one in the leakage direction** beyond #914. The SOFR complex (rate, percentiles, volume) is verified never restated across five ALFRED vintage pairs back to 2019-04-01, which simplifies #940's historical check.

### 3.2 Settlement mechanics, and which day's data reflects which day's pressure

Mechanics, with sources:

- **SOFR(d) is priced on d and settles on d.** The NY Fed defines overnight transactions as "settled on the same day as the trade date and maturing the following business day" (reference-rates information page, verified). Tri-party rates for d are largely agreed between ~07:00 and ~10:00 on d (MMF cash is placed in the morning; **suspected**, desk knowledge, no public page states it); GCF and DVP trade through the afternoon. So SOFR(d) is the funding pressure of d, and a settlement on d is a same-day driver — the repo's scheduled read at row T is the right alignment.
- **Fedwire Funds:** opens 21:00 ET the preceding calendar day; cutoffs 17:00 (tax payments/special accounts), 18:45 (customer transfers), 19:00 (bank transfers and close) per https://www.frbservices.org/resources/financial-services/wires/operating-hours.html — **verified**; note the page's 18:45/19:00 differ from the brief's 18:30. **Fedwire Securities:** business day opens 08:00, transfers from 08:30, secondary-market transfer cutoff **15:15**, reversals **15:30**, application closes 19:00 (https://www.frbservices.org/resources/financial-services/securities/operating-hours.html) — **verified**. Treasury issue-date deliveries go out at the open and are paid for in the morning of d, so the TGA rises and reserves fall on d (**suspected** as to the morning timing; the DTS for d will show it at 16:00 on d+1).
- **ON RRP:** 12:45–13:15; "Counterparties must deliver funds by 4:30 p.m. ET"; cash moves from the clearing bank to the NY Fed, reducing reserves the same afternoon (RRP FAQ, verified). ON RRP(d) is therefore a same-afternoon drain and a contemporaneous read on how much MMF cash found no private repo at or above the RRP rate on the morning of d.
- **SRF:** afternoon operation settles "within thirty minutes following the operation close time" in BNY tri-party (repo FAQ, verified). Take-up on d relieves d's afternoon, after the tri-party leg of SOFR(d) is priced.
- **Tri-party and FICC:** since the 2016 reform there is no morning unwind; BNY settles tri-party in the afternoon with rolling substitution; FICC nets DVP by CUSIP and settles on Fedwire Securities before the 15:15 cutoff; GCF settles on the BNY platform (all **suspected**, desk knowledge, not re-verified today). The GCF index can exist same day (~15:30) because the GCF day is over; SOFR waits for the DVP and tri-party files overnight.

At 16:00 on T−1 a desk therefore holds, dated T−1: ON RRP (13:15+), SRF (08:30+, 13:45+), auction results (11:30/13:00+), the DTCC GCF index (15:30), and the OFR cleared rates for T−2 (15:00); dated T−2: SOFR (08:00 on T−1), the DTS TGA (16:00 on T−1, at the tie). The published declaration reads SOFR(T−2) and nothing dated T−1 except the calendar and the settlement schedule.

**Off-by-one audit (beyond #914).** I walked `declared_availability` (`asof.py:216-260`) and `_latest` (`:534-553`) against each declaration on an ordinary week and a holiday week:

- Daily NY Fed rates: row T−2 at a T−1 decision — correct (`asof.py:9-13`, docstring; verified by construction, `published = position + days` on panel rows, `:255-259`).
- FR 2004 at +6 panel days: the Wednesday row is read from the Friday after the Thursday print, and the Tuesday row (previous week's level) at the Thursday decision — correct; a Good Friday in the gap adds one conservative day.
- H.4.1 at +5 calendar days from each *row* (rows carry the Wednesday level forward, `docs/decisions/weekly-carry.md`): a Monday decision reads the row dated the previous Tuesday, i.e. the Wednesday twelve days back, one print older than the public one — the known two-panel-day staleness; `_observation_available` (`asof.py:568-591`) correctly ages it from the Wednesday. Not a leak; ruled (#541).
- H.8 at +12 calendar days: one panel day conservative; no leak.
- OFR at +2 business days: one panel day conservative for DVP/GCF (item B); exact for tri-party.
- DTS at 16:30: conservative by a day at the 16:00 tie.
- `net_settlement` sits on the settlement's own row and `net_settlement_due_5d` on the five after (`src/repo_model/net_settlement.py:17-23`): the "decision day's settlement, not the scored day's" case is #568/#618 (tracked, fixed at h=1).
- Settlement `scheduled_availability` at 15:00 the panel day before: every auction settling inside the panel closed by 14:30 and at least one panel day earlier (`sources.json:806`); no leak.
- IORB at 16:15 on its own date (`:141`): the spread for T uses IORB(T) only in the outcome; the input features read the announced table (`announced_iorb`); no leak.
- `quarter_end` on the last SOFR-publication weekday: the overnight trade of that day spans the statement date — correct for the turn.

## 4. Briefs

### A. DTCC GCF Repo Index as a same-day input (HIGH)

**What and why.** The GCF Repo Index is the par-weighted average rate of overnight GCF Repo trades submitted to FICC, Treasury class (CUSIP 371487AE9) and MBS class, with total par in $bn, published since November 2010 "about 3:30 p.m." each business day on dtcc.com and Bloomberg GCFR (fact sheet, verified). It is the only public repo rate for day T−1 that exists at the T−1 16:00 decision. GCF is the interdealer GC market that reprices first when dealer balance sheets are constrained; GCF minus tri-party (TGCR) is the intermediation premium. The repo already found that the mere *presence* of the OFR GCF value tracks pressure (#520) — that presence is OFR disclosure suppression lifting when volume rises, i.e. a shadow of the signal the full index carries every day.

**Point-in-time implementation.** Series: DTCC GCF Repo Index, Treasury (rate, par volume).
