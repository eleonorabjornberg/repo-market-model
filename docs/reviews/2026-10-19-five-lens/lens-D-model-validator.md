**Verdict in one line.** Not approvable for any desk use today, which the project itself states (`docs/use-limitation.md:10`; `docs/model/validation.md:23`). Both are fixable without new data.

## 3. Conceptual soundness

**What the candidate is (verified).** `gbm_funding` = one `HistGradientBoostingRegressor(loss="quantile")` per level at 23 levels (`src/repo_model/ml.py:22-24, 2821-2835`), fitted on one-step pairs, row *i*'s spread on row *i−1*'s spread and regressors (`src/repo_model/baseline.py:1779-1782`), uncalibrated (`docs/model/validation.md:117,125`), refit every 21 scored days with minimum history 61 (`validation.md:123`), on the nine features of the published declaration: `reserve_balances, sofr_p25, sofr_p75, sofr_volume, spread_bps, tbill_13w, tbill_4w, tga, treasury_settlement` (`scripts/final_test_preregistration.py:837-838`). Beyond `early_stopping=False`, `random_state` and `min_samples_leaf=20`, every hyperparameter is scikit-learn's default (`ml.py:2823-2833`): 100 iterations, learning rate 0.1, 31 leaves, unbounded depth, no L2. Quantile crossing is fixed by sorting (`ml.py:34-46`), a valid rearrangement. The module docstring itself states that the in-sample fit puts the 0.05–0.95 band "at well under" 90% (`ml.py:56-59`).

**Is the economic story behind each input documented?** Partly. `contract.py:274-334` gives one-line reasons for the splice of IORB/IOER, the settlement split, and the bill maturities ("the 4-week bill is money funds' nearest substitute for overnight repo"). `DATA.md:47-62` gives units only. Neither the validation report nor `METHODOLOGY.md` states, per input, the sign a practitioner expects, the lag at which the mechanism acts, or why the raw level is the right encoding. Under SR 11-7 that is the gap in "documentation ... of model limitations and assumptions". Input by input:

- **`spread_bps` (lagged spread).** Sound and dominant. Read at the row two panel days before T at h=1 (`docs/decisions/information-set.md:30-31`), because SOFR for day T−1 prints the morning of T (NY Fed: published one business day after the value date, revised only the same day at ~14:30 ET if the change exceeds 1 bp; https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/additional-information-about-reference-rates). The model, however, is *trained* on the one-step pair (`baseline.py:1779-1782`) and *served* at a two-step gap at h=1 and up to six at h=5. A one-step law under-reverts when served at a longer gap, and the spread's spikes are upward, so the centre sits too high after a spike. **Verified consequence:** outcomes fall below the published model's median 58.9% of the time (6-bin PIT computed from `docs/runs/backtest_gbm_conformal_pid_nested_funding.json`: 0.051/0.362/0.176/0.143/0.207/0.061 against nominal 0.05/0.20/0.25/0.25/0.20/0.05; the repo's own histogram agrees, `docs/diagnosis-interior-calibration.md:46-52`), and 61.7% for the uncalibrated gbm (`docs/runs/backtest_gbm.json`). The 2021-23 diagnosis reads the same way: "too high and too narrow", median bias −0.39 bp (`docs/pivot/h5-step3-diagnoses-result.md:14-17`). The direct-pairs remedy is tracked (#449/#825/#865/#885/#931).
- **`sofr_p25`, `sofr_p75` (SOFR's 25th/75th percentiles, in percent).** The mechanism is sound: dispersion widens before pressure. The encoding is not: a percentile *level* in percent tracks the policy rate; the economic quantity is the width `p75 − SOFR` and `SOFR − p25` in bp. Verified: correlation of `p75 − SOFR` with the next-day spread is +0.20 to +0.53 in every regime (computed), a stable sign; the level's information is mostly the rate cycle. Trees can recover the difference only through many interacting splits on two levels that drift from 1.7% to 5.4% across the sample.
- **`sofr_volume` ($bn).** Rose from $700bn (2018) to $3.5tn (2026) (computed from the panel). A level feature with a trend has no stationary meaning; every 2026 value is outside the 2018-23 training support, and trees extrapolate flat. The validation report flags the clearing-mandate drift of this input (`validation.md:225`) but not the trend already in the sample.
- **`reserve_balances` (FRED `WRESBAL`).** This is the H.4.1 *week average* for the week ending Wednesday (FRED series title, verified: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/WRESBAL), published Thursdays "generally at 4:30 p.m." (https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h41/), read per release (`information-set.md:30-35`). The sign is believable (−0.25 to −0.64 with next-day spread in four of five regimes, computed), but the magnitude is not transferable: reserves were $1.39–1.72tn during the 2019 stress and $2.85–3.48tn during the 2025 stress (computed). A tree learns "below $1.7tn → spike" from 2019 and the rule never fires again. The repo knows this (#569: "The point where reserves become scarce has moved since 2019"; H7's ratio to bank assets, #587/#598/#626), yet the candidate reads the raw level and the scarcity state is "not a model input" (`validation.md:204`).
- **`tga` (FRED `WTREGEN`).** Also a week average (verified, FRED). The documented mechanism is the tax-date drain, which happens over two or three days; a week average published the following Thursday cannot carry it at h=1–5. What the model actually reads is a slow level. Daily DTS was tested in #763 (already raised); the validator's point is different: the *documented* story and the *read* quantity disagree, so the "no gain" from DTS cannot be read as "the TGA mechanism has no signal" (#951 asks the same about calendar inputs).
- **`tbill_4w`, `tbill_13w` (coupon-equivalent levels).** The practitioner's mechanism is bill cheapness relative to the repo/IORB complex pulling MMF cash out of repo, i.e. the sign of `bill − IORB` (or `bill − ON RRP award`). **Verified sign instability of the level:** correlation with the next-day spread +0.12 (2018-19), +0.18 (2020), +0.09 (2021-23), −0.46 (2024), −0.41 (2025-26). The level in 2024-26 is a proxy for "the Fed has cut", not for bill supply. In the 2023 debt-ceiling window the 4-week bill reached 5.90% against IORB 5.15% (+75 bp, 2023-05-26) while SOFR − IORB stayed at −9 to −10 bp (computed from the panel): the input moved far outside its training support with no effect on the target, which a validator reads as a feature that cannot be interpreted in the model. Levels in 2020 touched −0.04%.
- **`treasury_settlement` (gross, $bn).** Gross, not net of maturities (Lens A); dropped at h≥2 when read as published (`scripts/live_record.py:190-199`); the one-day training offset is #914 (already raised; guard #947).
- **Calendar flags.** `quarter_end`, `tax_date`, `days_to_month_end` are in the panel but **not** among the candidate's inputs (`final_test_preregistration.py:837-838`): a 1–5-day model of a market whose largest moves fall on dates known weeks ahead reads no calendar; B2 does. Part of #951's audit.
- **Not read:** ON RRP (declared off, `contract.py:336-346`), SRF take-up, dealer positions. Acceptable, but the validation report's §2 should say so.

**Which inputs would a practitioner not believe?** The bill levels (sign flips with the cycle), the reserves level (the 2019 cut-point cannot recur), the volume level (trend), and the TGA week average standing in for a tax-date drain. None is wrong in direction when encoded relatively; all are wrong as raw levels in trees with 31 leaves and unbounded depth on 60–1,900 rows.

**What to do instead.** `contract.py:321-322` declares bill yields "as published ... documented, not converted". A practitioner would declare `tbill_4w − iorb` and `tbill_13w − iorb` in bp (same publication instant, 16:30 ET per the registry as rendered in `validation.md:79`; no new source), `sofr_p75 − sofr` and `sofr − sofr_p25` in bp, `sofr_volume ÷ its trailing 60-row median` (causal), and reserves as the as-of ratio already built for H7: one declared variant under #634's machinery.

### 4.3 Why the candidate choice is the problem

The protocol picks by grid CRPS alone and calls coverage "reported, not decision rules" (`scoring-protocol.md:194-197`); H5 step 5 chose "by the protocol score alone" (`validation.md:33`). CRPS is proper but rewards sharpness, so between a calibrated law and a narrower mis-calibrated one it can prefer the narrow one. That is what happened: the candidate's CRPS edge over B3 is −0.9% [−2.7%, +0.8%] (`spread-distribution-protocol-result.md:36`) with 0.695 coverage against B3's 0.886. SR 11-7 asks for tests "designed for each situation"; for a quantile forecast that is coverage, by lead and regime, as a pass requirement. The fix is a §13 amendment of kind (a): H1's synthetic suite can show in a day that the selection rule ranks an under-dispersed synthetic forecast above a calibrated one.

**Does the design support them?** The *record* does: one frozen file per business day, written after 16:00 ET and before any outcome, chained, digests posted to issue #225 and anchored in Rekor, raw inputs archived, missed days recorded as missed and never back-filled (`live-log.yml:1-45`); B1's law frozen per record (`docs/decisions/live-scoring-declaration.md:39-46`); from record version 3, B1/B2 at 23 levels and every registered candidate (`src/repo_model/prospective_log.py:1-16`; `REGISTERED_CANDIDATES` empty, line 53); a block written after the outcome's first print is refused (`prospective_log.py:17-22`). The *reader* does not: (a) `live_score.py` may run only on 2027-04-01, 2027-10-01 and each 1 October (`live_score.py:6-12,299-310`) and refuses any day the lockbox has not opened, today every day after 2026-09-03 (`metadata/lockbox.json`; `live-score.yml:21-23`); (b) it computes CRPS and Brier cells only (`live_score.py:399-491,588-640`); (c) the record has no per-field staleness or imputation block (`live_record.py:1307-1315` carries snapshot digests, `panel_last_date` and three file hashes); (d) the candidate is not logged until registered (`validation.md:282`). The obstacle is the lockbox's wording, "no comparison scores a locked day" (`src/repo_model/lockbox.py:1-14`). A bank separates *monitoring* from *evidence*: monitoring reads outcomes as they print, opens no tier, carries no verdict, and is cited only as a trigger. That needs one decision record and one daily job; the lockbox and the verdict machinery stay untouched. Until then the earliest date anyone can learn that the live model is mis-calibrated is 2027-04-01.

**Minimum cell size.** `MINIMUM_CELL_DAYS = 20`, proposed not decided (`live_score.py:128-134`; `live-scoring-declaration.md:67-73`). For a quarter-end cell that is four to five years; a turn-day trigger must pool turns across the whole record.

## 6. Limitations and use

**The use limitation I would sign** (replacing `docs/use-limitation.md:10` and `validation.md:23` for the candidate):

> `gbm_funding` and the published distribution are development-stage research forecasts of SOFR − IORB in bp, 1–5 business days ahead. **Not approved for any use that carries risk:** not for limits, VaR, liquidity or capital, collateral or cash-position sizing, pricing, or as a stress indicator.
